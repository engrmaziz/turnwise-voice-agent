# Hours

Turnwise Clinic is open Monday through Friday nine to five. We are closed on weekends.

# Walk-ins

We take walk-ins when a slot is free. Booked visits keep their slot.

# Location

Turnwise Clinic is at 100 Example Street. Parking is behind the building.

# Referral

You do not need a referral for a general visit. Specialist visits may ask for one.
